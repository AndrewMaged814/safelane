# Eligibility is not risk

**Superseded by [0003-risk-selects-the-lane](0003-risk-selects-the-lane.md).** Risk-based
envelopes are no longer a future extension — they are built. This ADR's claim below
still holds and is restated there: evidence completeness is not risk.

GitHub, CI, and registry checks decide Release Eligibility, not risk. Evidence completeness does not produce low, medium, or high risk and does not select a Release Lane. ADR 0003 superseded only the former static-envelope consequence by adding Change Assessment and risk-shaped lanes.
